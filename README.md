# 💫 About Me

# Systems Architect & Quantitative Researcher

I am currently engineering a **Probabilistic Trading System**, a high-performance research platform designed to bridge the gap between statistical rigor and real-world market execution. My approach treats financial intelligence as a holistic system rather than a collection of isolated models.

---

### 🛠 Technical Core
* **Hybrid Modeling:** I combine **Deep Learning time-series models** for price forecasting with **Probabilistic models** (specifically Markov chains) to quantify directional movement and regime shifts.
* **AI Systems Engineering:** I focus on the entire lifecycle—from low-latency data ingestion and feature engineering to model orchestration and deployment-ready pipelines.
* **Performance Tech Stack:** Leveraging **Python** for rapid modeling and **C++20** for performance-critical components, with **DuckDB** serving as the high-speed analytical backbone for market data.

### 🎯 Specializations
* **Financial RAG:** Implementing Retrieval-Augmented Generation to turn unstructured financial data into actionable intelligence.
* **Multi-Agent Architectures:** Building systems where autonomous agents integrate predictive models, long-term memory, and decision logic into a coherent execution strategy using frameworks like **LangGraph**.
* **Decision Usefulness:** My design philosophy prioritizes **interpretability and robustness** over "blind" backtest accuracy. In a stochastic environment, I value a system’s ability to handle tail risks and regime changes.

> "The goal isn't just to predict the next tick; it's to build a robust system that understands the probability of its own success."

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 📈 Probabilistic Trading System
A flagship, high-performance algorithmic trading infrastructure architected for the Indian equity markets. 
* **Engineered in C++20** for ultra-low latency execution and high-throughput market data processing, optimizing processing latency by 76%.
* **Utilizes LangGraph** to orchestrate parallelized, stateful multi-agent workflows for real-time Sentiment Analysis and VIX ingestion.
* **Integrates hybrid Python/C++ bindings** via `pybind11` to combine heavy-duty computational backends with flexible, high-level orchestration logic.

### 🧮 QuantEngine (In Development)
A comprehensive, production-grade Python package translating the core mathematical frameworks of derivative pricing and financial engineering into optimized code.
* Built as a programmatic, highly modular realization of advanced quantitative finance principles (inspired by John C. Hull’s *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives*).
* Implements structural options pricing models, interest rate derivatives, and risk management analytics.
